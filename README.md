A 100-word monologue from a dark lord who is secretly just very tired — written by a small Node program to /output/entry.txt. The tiredness lives in the voice, the rhythm and the small deflections, never in a plain statement; the speaker is still a villain with a scheme, a grudge and a history.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

The program writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` (set `OUTPUT_DIR` to write elsewhere) and asserts the gate before writing: exactly 100 words, 1–600 characters, and no word that simply announces the tiredness.

## The monologue

> Come in. The door locks itself — one of the few spells that still works.
>
> You want the crown, the throne, the map room. Take a number. Three heroes are queuing in the courtyard; I gave them blankets. It rains through the roof, and the steward holds the umbrella over the treasury.
>
> I built this empire on four hours of sleep and a grudge against my brother. The grudge has retired. The sleep has not returned.
>
> Kneel, or don't. The floor is cold either way. I have rehearsed your death three times today — twice while waiting for the kettle.
